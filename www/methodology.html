Methodology

Public GIS pre-screens. It does not close title.

Every Available screen states its basis. When a rule cannot be proven from public record, the lead says so instead of inventing confidence.

How screens work

  • Flood, wetlands, and habitat are evaluated against parcel geometry when polygons exist.
  • Soil build scores come from USDA SSURGO and list every deduction in plain language.
  • Buildable acres use setback envelopes on city lots and hydric/SFHA deductions on tracts.
  • Road access is physical (TIGER/Line). Recorded easements still require plat and title.
  • Water/sewer may be confirmed (district polygon), likely (territory table), or not verifiable.

What public data cannot settle

  • Legal access via private easement
  • Deed restrictions and HOA covenants
  • Will-serve capacity from a utility
  • Phase I environmental conclusions
  • ALTA survey encroachments
  • Interconnection queue position or LMP power economics

Territory “likely” is not the same as measured distance to a main or transmission line. In-build HIFLD distance-to-line upgrades power answers when Task 2 ships.
